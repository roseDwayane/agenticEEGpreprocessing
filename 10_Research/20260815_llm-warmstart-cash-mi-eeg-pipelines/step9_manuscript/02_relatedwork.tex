\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Our work sits at the intersection of four strands: CASH and AutoML systems, transfer and warm-started hyperparameter optimization, LLM-assisted optimization, and automated or subject-adaptive EEG decoding. We review each in turn and close by contrasting our approach with its nearest neighbors.

\subsection{CASH, AutoML Systems, and Benchmarking}
\label{sec:related:cash}

Auto-WEKA introduced the CASH formulation, jointly searching algorithm choices and hyperparameters with model-based optimization \cite{KotthoffEtAl2019}, and subsequent systems industrialized the idea: Hyperopt and its sklearn integration \cite{BergstraEtAl2015}, TPOT's tree-based genetic programming over full pipelines \cite{OlsonEtAl2016, OlsonMoore2019}, Auto-Keras for architecture search \cite{JinEtAl2019}, AutoGluon's stacking-centric design \cite{EricksonEtAl2020}, and general-purpose engines such as SMAC3 \cite{LindauerEtAl2021}, Optuna \cite{AkibaEtAl2019}, and the OpenBox service \cite{LiEtAl2021a}. Multi-fidelity methods trade evaluation fidelity for breadth \cite{LiEtAl2016, FalknerEtAl2018, KleinEtAl2016}. As search spaces grew, structure became the differentiator: VolcanoML decomposes CASH into tree-structured execution plans whose advantage over monolithic optimizers widens with dimensionality \cite{LiEtAl2021b, LiEtAl2022a}, Rising Bandits eliminate weak algorithm arms with non-stationary bandits, maintaining 95.02\% accuracy as candidate algorithms scale from one to sixteen while monolithic SMAC degrades, at roughly 12.6$\times$ fewer trials on average \cite{LiEtAl2020a}, and related decompositions appear in ADMM-based formulations \cite{LiuEtAl2019} and hierarchical planning \cite{WeverEtAl2021}. Surveys consolidate this landscape \cite{HutterEtAl2019, ZollerHuber2019, ElshawiEtAl2019, HeEtAl2019, BarbudoEtAl2023}.

The benchmarking substrate matters as much as the systems. OpenML-based suites \cite{GijsbersEtAl2019, GijsbersEtAl2021}, HPOBench \cite{EggenspergerEtAl2021}, and HPO-B \cite{ArangoEtAl2021} standardize comparisons, and their authors flag anytime-performance reporting, meta-learning train/test contamination, and transfer-HPO task families as open problems \cite{GijsbersEtAl2019, EggenspergerEtAl2021}. These cautions are earned: across 114 datasets, CASH optimizers were statistically indistinguishable on average and random search was not reliably worse \cite{ZollerHuber2019}. Our evaluation protocol adopts these lessons wholesale---budget matching, anytime curves, and strict separation between prior learning and evaluation---and our released per-subject search histories are designed to serve as exactly the realistic transfer task family this community lacks.

\subsection{Warm-Starting and Transfer in Bayesian Optimization}
\label{sec:related:transfer}

Transfer HPO reuses optimization knowledge across tasks; surveys organize the mechanisms by injection point \cite{BaiEtAl2023, Vanschoren2019}. At the \emph{initial-design} level, meta-feature-matched warm-starting selects the first configurations from similar past datasets---the gain over cold-started SMAC exceeded SMAC's own gain over random search \cite{FeurerEtAl2015}---and learned defaults \cite{PfistererEtAl2018}, collaborative filtering over algorithm-dataset matrices \cite{YangEtAl2018}, and zero-shot data-dependent configuration \cite{GijsbersEtAl2021, HollmannEtAl2025} extend the idea. Initial-design composition itself measurably shapes downstream model-based search \cite{BossekEtAl2020}. At the \emph{surrogate} level, two-stage transfer surrogates weight source models by task similarity \cite{WistubaGrabocka2021, LiEtAl2022c}, with learned optimizers pushing further \cite{ChenEtAl2022}; at the \emph{search-space} level, promising regions are carved from history \cite{LiEtAl2022d, PerroneEtAl2019}. At the \emph{acquisition} level, $\pi$BO injects a decaying prior over optima, achieving up to 12.5$\times$ faster time-to-accuracy from a default-centered prior and provably recovering from wrong priors \cite{HvarfnerEtAl2022}.

Two robustness results shape our design. Multi-task Gaussian-process surrogates misestimate cross-task correlation even for affinely related tasks, making surrogate-level transfer structurally fragile \cite{HvarfnerEtAl2026}; and similarity-gated warm-starting with a fallback to cold start guards against low-similarity sources \cite{NomuraEtAl2021}. Given the documented magnitude of inter-subject EEG variability \cite{ZhangEtAl2024}, we therefore inject knowledge at the initial-design and acquisition-prior levels---never the surrogate---and gate the injection by subject meta-feature similarity.

\subsection{LLMs in the Optimization Loop}
\label{sec:related:llm}

LLAMBO demonstrated LLMs as zero-shot warm-starters, surrogates, and candidate samplers for BO, with warm-starting the most robust component \cite{LiuEtAl2024}; follow-ups probe when domain pretraining is required \cite{KristiadiEtAl2024}, hybridize LLMs with classical engines \cite{MahammadliErtekin2024, XuEtAl2026b}, and refine per-iteration preference injection \cite{YuanEtAl2026}. Beyond hyperparameters, LLMs configure software tools \cite{Kannan2023}, tune solvers instance-specifically \cite{WangEtAl2025}, and drive feature-engineering-conditioned CASH: CoFEH interleaves LLM feature engineering with FE-conditioned SMAC, reaching average rank 1.75 with up to 45.1\% error reduction and raising surrogate rank correlation from 0.587 to 0.691 \cite{XuEtAl2026a}. Prior elicitation itself is emerging as the cleanest interface: language-derived priors feed $\pi$BO-style mechanisms \cite{TopalisEtAl2025}.

The corrective literature is equally important. An independent reproduction with open Llama-3.1-70B confirmed LLAMBO's contextual warm-start effect but found sub-70B backbones produce malformed or uncorrelated outputs \cite{RychertEtAl2025}. A budget-matched audit showed that in several LLM-advisor loops the ``warm start'' is a fixed default evaluated before any model call, the LLM's marginal contribution is statistically null, and seeded classical search overtakes within twelve evaluations \cite{RodriguesEtAl2026}; LLM advantages also fade outside low-dimensional settings \cite{SrinivasanMenzies2026} and are sensitive to prompt protocol \cite{LeiCooper2026, GuptaEtAl2025}. We inherit these constraints as design choices: API-scale backbones only, fixed released prompts, and a seeded cold-start control that isolates prior-shape guidance from first-configuration quality (H3).

\subsection{Automated and Subject-Adaptive EEG Decoding}
\label{sec:related:eeg}

Automated design in EEG decoding is real but narrow. Evolutionary and swarm methods dominate: differential evolution outperformed TPE for emotion-recognition hyperparameters \cite{NakisaEtAl2018}, genetic algorithms tuned shallow CNNs to match deeper networks at far lower cost \cite{LeonEtAl2020}, and cross-task NAS \cite{DuanEtAl2023}, hardware-aware NAS with 89\% search-time reduction and +8.70\% accuracy \cite{ZhuEtAl2025}, filter-bank NAS for subject-adaptive decoding \cite{WangEtAl2026}, and hierarchical NAS for cross-subject emotion recognition \cite{HuEtAl2019, AvvalEtAl2025} extend the line. Genetic programming has also assembled full EEG pipelines \cite{MirandaEtAl2022}. Notably absent are the BO-based CASH engines of Section~\ref{sec:related:cash}, and the statistical significance of hyperparameter effects in deep EEG decoding remains largely untested \cite{CooneyEtAl2020}.

Cross-subject adaptation, meanwhile, transfers representations rather than configurations: deep transfer learning \cite{SchirrmeisterEtAl2017, BirdEtAl2020, HuangEtAl2025}, domain adaptation and meta-learning for zero-calibration decoding \cite{AristimunhaEtAl2025}, and EEG-Reptile's automated meta-learning library, whose few-shot ceiling (43$\pm$7\% zero-shot, 46$\pm$5\% with sixteen samples on four-class MI) contrasts with roughly 84\% for full subject-specific training \cite{BerdyshevEtAl2024, SchirrmeisterEtAl2017}---and whose automated HPO costs about twenty-four hours per setting \cite{BerdyshevEtAl2024}. Closest to our secondary aim, personalized classifier selection predicts a classifier from dataset structural characteristics \cite{AnarakiEtAl2024}, cross-subject channel selection transfers one configuration dimension \cite{WeiEtAl2023}, and metaheuristic HPO for cross-subject emotion recognition restarts cold per dataset \cite{ZhangEtAl2024}. LLM agents have entered BCI tooling for analysis automation, with LLM-driven HPO/NAS named as future work \cite{KapitonovaBall2024}.

% --- Final contrast paragraph ---

Our approach differs from the closest prior work in three respects. Unlike EEG-Reptile \cite{BerdyshevEtAl2024}, which transfers meta-learned \emph{weights} and leaves hyperparameter search cold, we transfer \emph{search knowledge}---histories and priors over the full pipeline configuration space. Unlike personalized classifier selection \cite{AnarakiEtAl2024}, which recommends a single component without a search loop, we warm-start a decomposed CASH search that continues to adapt to the target subject. And unlike LLM-advisor systems audited by \cite{RodriguesEtAl2026}, we treat the LLM strictly as one of two interchangeable prior sources within a gated $\pi$BO mechanism \cite{HvarfnerEtAl2022}, evaluated against the seeded baseline that their audit shows is necessary. To our knowledge, no prior work combines these elements, and none evaluates configuration-level cross-subject transfer at all.

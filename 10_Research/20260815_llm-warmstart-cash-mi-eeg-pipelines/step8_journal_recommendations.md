## Submission Strategy / 投稿策略

If the results emphasize the *BCI calibration-cost* story (H1+H2 strong on EEG metrics): submit JNE first; on rejection, TNSRE with reviewer-response-informed revisions, then CIBM. If the results emphasize the *knowledge-injection methodology* (H3 ablations most interesting; LLM-prior arm strong): lead with AEI, positioning EEG as the demanding real-world task family; fall back to CIBM, then JBHI. The anytime-benchmark asset (GAP_003) can be spun off as a short data/benchmark paper regardless of the main paper's venue.

若結果主打「BCI 校正成本」敘事（H1+H2 在 EEG 指標上強）：先投 JNE，被拒後依審稿意見修訂轉 TNSRE、再 CIBM。若結果主打「知識注入方法學」（H3 消融最有趣、LLM 先驗臂強）：以 AEI 為首選，把 EEG 定位為嚴苛的真實任務家族；備案 CIBM、JBHI。無論主論文投何處，anytime 基準資產（GAP_003）皆可獨立拆出為短篇 data/benchmark 論文。

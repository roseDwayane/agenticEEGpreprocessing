\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Our work sits at the intersection of three research strands that have, to date, developed independently: automated EEG preprocessing, spectral parameterization of neural power, and pipeline/hyperparameter optimization (from classical AutoML to LLM agents). We review each in turn and close by contrasting our approach with the closest prior work.

\subsection{Automated EEG preprocessing pipelines}
\label{sec:rw_preproc}

The standardization of EEG preprocessing began with the PREP pipeline, which formalized robust referencing and bad-channel detection for large-scale data~\cite{BigdelyShamloEtAl2015}. Subsequent systems wrapped a now-canonical operation sequence --- filtering, line-noise removal, ICA decomposition, automated component classification, bad-segment correction, and re-referencing --- into turnkey pipelines targeting specific populations and recording conditions. The HAPPE family addresses developmental and high-artifact data and its low-density and event-related variants~\cite{GabardDurnamEtAl2018, LopezEtAl2022, MonachinoEtAl2022}; NEAR targets neonatal recordings~\cite{KumaravelEtAl2022}; APPEAR handles the gradient and ballistocardiogram artifacts of simultaneous EEG-fMRI~\cite{MayeliEtAl2021}; and DISCOVER-EEG extends automation through to resting-state feature extraction~\cite{vilaEtAl2023}. Automagic explicitly couples preprocessing with a standardized quality-assessment report and BIDS compatibility~\cite{PedroniEtAl2019}.

Several component methods are reused across these pipelines. ICLabel provides automated classification of independent components into brain, muscle, eye, heart, line-noise, channel-noise, and other categories~\cite{PionTonachiniEtAl2019}, now available in Python via MNE-ICALabel~\cite{LiEtAl2022}. Autoreject estimates per-sensor rejection thresholds by cross-validation~\cite{JasEtAl2017}; Artifact Subspace Reconstruction, including its Riemannian variant, repairs transient high-variance segments~\cite{BlumEtAl2019}; and the SOUND algorithm suppresses noise using a data-driven sensor-noise estimate~\cite{MutanenEtAl2018}. What unites this body of work is that each pipeline applies a \emph{fixed} configuration: parameters are set to defaults, not searched per recording, and the field still lacks an agreed objective for comparing configurations~\cite{PedroniEtAl2019}.

\subsection{Spectral parameterization and the aperiodic component}
\label{sec:rw_fooof}

The separation of neural power spectra into periodic and aperiodic components has become a standard analysis. The FOOOF/specparam algorithm models a spectrum as a $1/f$-like aperiodic background plus Gaussian oscillatory peaks~\cite{HallerEtAl2018, DonoghueEtAl2020b}, while IRASA achieves a related separation by resampling~\cite{WenLiu2016}. The two methods differ in their failure modes, which \citet{GersterEtAl2022} characterize across EEG, MEG, and LFP data, providing concrete guidance --- fitting-range choice, knee handling, and overlapping-peak detection --- that any metric built on FOOOF must respect. The importance of explicit parameterization is underscored by \citet{DonoghueEtAl2020a}, who demonstrate that conventional band-ratio measures conflate periodic and aperiodic changes.

A substantial literature establishes that the aperiodic component is itself meaningful signal rather than nuisance. The spectral slope tracks arousal and brain state~\cite{LendnerEtAl2020}; aperiodic exponent and offset change with development and aging~\cite{HillEtAl2022, MerkinEtAl2022}; periodic and aperiodic components link to distinct aspects of cognition across the lifespan~\cite{ThuwalEtAl2021}; and time-resolved parameterization tracks their fluctuation within a recording~\cite{WilsonEtAl2022}. Practical guidance for applying parameterization in developmental contexts is given by \citet{OstlundEtAl2022}. This validated status is precisely why our metric must distinguish aperiodic \emph{physiology} from aperiodic \emph{contamination} rather than minimizing aperiodic power wholesale. Notably, although these studies use FOOOF descriptively --- to report exponents and offsets --- none uses it as an optimization objective for preprocessing.

\subsection{Objective preprocessing quality and sensitivity}
\label{sec:rw_quality}

The question of how to judge preprocessing without a downstream task is recent and sparsely addressed. \citet{RobbinsEtAl2020} quantify how much amplitude-domain EEG measures vary with preprocessing choices, establishing the sensitivity that motivates principled selection. \citet{Delorme2023} goes further, introducing a label-light quality metric and using it to compare optimized pipelines across EEGLAB, FieldTrip, MNE, and Brainstorm; the striking finding that many automated steps fail to help, or harm, both motivates the need for an objective target and serves as a cautionary baseline. \citet{CoelliEtAl2023} perform a manual, per-step objective comparison and propose quantitative selection indices. Our FOOOF-SNR differs from \citet{Delorme2023}'s metric in being task- and label-free and applicable to single resting-state recordings, and it differs from \citet{CoelliEtAl2023}'s manual comparison by being the objective of an automated search.

\subsection{Pipeline optimization: from AutoML to LLM agents}
\label{sec:rw_optim}

Casting pipeline construction as search has a long history in AutoML. Auto-WEKA and Auto-sklearn jointly select algorithms and hyperparameters via Bayesian optimization~\cite{ThorntonEtAl2012, FeurerEtAl2015, FeurerEtAl2020}, with modern hyperparameter-optimization practice surveyed by \citet{BischlEtAl2023} and adaptive-experiment Bayesian optimization reviewed by \citet{GreenhillEtAl2020}; broader perspectives on the field's trajectory and evaluation appear in~\cite{SantuEtAl2020, CooneyEtAl2020}. Critically, this machinery has been extended to data-side decisions: \citet{NeutatzEtAl2022} ask whether an optimizer would choose to clean data, and \citet{SiddiqiEtAl2023} optimize data-cleaning pipelines for downstream ML. In the EEG domain specifically, \citet{BashashatiEtAl2016} applied Bayesian optimization to per-user BCI pipelines, and \citet{MartinezEtAl2023} search per-instance preprocessing pipelines --- the work closest to ours in spirit, though for generic data and optimizing task loss rather than a spectral quality metric.

The current frontier replaces fixed search with LLM agents that reason, use tools, and explore. Multi-agent and tool-orchestration substrates~\cite{WuEtAl2023, ShenEtAl2023} and benchmarks for LLM ML-engineering agents~\cite{HuangEtAl2023} have enabled a wave of data-science agents: LLM-driven feature engineering~\cite{HollmannEtAl2023}, case-based-reasoning data-science agents~\cite{GuoEtAl2024}, end-to-end interpreters~\cite{HongEtAl2024}, and search-augmented variants using tree search and Monte-Carlo tree search~\cite{JiangEtAl2025, ChiEtAl2024, TriratEtAl2024}, alongside earlier GPT-driven AutoML systems~\cite{ZhangEtAl2023, TsaiEtAl2023}. The autonomous-science archetypes --- Coscientist, ChemCrow, and the AI Scientist --- show LLM agents closing the full hypothesize--experiment--evaluate loop with domain tools~\cite{BoikoEtAl2023, BranEtAl2024, LuEtAl2024}, and a recent survey maps the design space of such agents~\cite{WangEtAl2024}. Across all of this work, however, the agents operate on tabular data, code, or chemistry; none has been applied to electrophysiological signal processing.

\subsection{Positioning of this work}
\label{sec:rw_position}

Our contribution is defined by the empty cell at the intersection of the four subsections above. Unlike the fixed pipelines of Section~\ref{sec:rw_preproc}, we search for a per-recording optimum; unlike the descriptive use of FOOOF in Section~\ref{sec:rw_fooof}, we make the periodic/aperiodic decomposition the optimization objective while explicitly guarding the physiologically real aperiodic component; unlike the label- or contrast-dependent metrics of Section~\ref{sec:rw_quality}, our FOOOF-SNR is task-free and applicable to single recordings; and unlike the optimizers and agents of Section~\ref{sec:rw_optim}, our controller operates on raw EEG and is, to our knowledge, the first LLM agent applied to neurophysiological preprocessing. The closest single prior work, \citet{MartinezEtAl2023}, establishes that per-instance preprocessing search is feasible but leaves both the EEG domain and the spectral-quality objective open --- precisely the territory this paper occupies.
